# UAE Local SEO Checklist

A working checklist for getting a Dubai or UAE business into the Google Map Pack and local search results. Maintained by the SEO team at [Digital Media Sapiens](https://digitalmediasapiens.com/), a Google Premier Partner agency in Dubai.

## 1. Google Business Profile
- [ ] Business name matches signage and trade licence exactly
- [ ] Primary category is the most specific one available
- [ ] Service areas set by emirate or community, not just "UAE"
- [ ] Opening hours updated for Ramadan and public holidays
- [ ] New photos uploaded monthly (exterior, interior, team, work)
- [ ] Products and services listed with short descriptions
- [ ] Q&A section seeded with real customer questions

## 2. NAP consistency
- [ ] Name, address and phone identical on the website, GBP and directories
- [ ] Same building or tower naming everywhere (e.g. "Cluster X, JLT")
- [ ] Local UAE landline shown, not only a mobile number

## 3. On-site
- [ ] One location page per area served, each with unique content
- [ ] LocalBusiness schema with geo coordinates
- [ ] Embedded map on the contact page
- [ ] Arabic versions of key pages with hreflang

## 4. Reviews
- [ ] Review request sent within 24 hours of service
- [ ] Every review answered, in the language it was written in
- [ ] No incentives offered for reviews (against Google policy)

## 5. Citations
- [ ] Listed on major UAE business directories and relevant industry directories
- [ ] Duplicate listings found and merged

## 6. Tracking
- [ ] GBP calls, direction requests and website clicks reviewed monthly
- [ ] UTM tag on the GBP website link so local traffic shows in GA4
